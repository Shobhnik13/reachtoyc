How it works

01

Pick a batch

Any YC batch, from the latest back to 2005. Choose how many companies, or only the ones hiring.

02

Find the founders

Names, roles, LinkedIn and open jobs from YC, with emails from company sites, smart guesses and Apify verification.

03

Research and write

Your own AI model reads up on each company and writes one personal email from your resume and your rules.

04

Review and send

Edit, regenerate, switch founder, copy or open in your mail app. Nothing goes out without you.

Why it's different

Your keys, your machine

Runs locally. API keys live in one .env file and never leave your backend.

Claude, OpenAI or Gemini

Bring whichever model you like; each one researches with its own web search.

Emails you can trust

Every address is labelled verified, unverified, on site or guess, so you know before you send.

Built for iterating

Regenerate the subject, the body or everything, with a line of extra context. Draft more anytime.

Any company, not just YC

Paste a website and the same research, email lookup and writing runs for that one company.

Agent-ready setup

Hand the repo to Claude Code or Codex: AGENTS.md walks them through every key and step.
